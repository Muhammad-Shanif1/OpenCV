🔍 What is approxPolyDP?

cv2.approxPolyDP() is a function in OpenCV that simplifies a shape (a curve or a contour) by reducing the number of points while keeping the shape roughly the same.

🧠 Why use it?

When you detect contours in an image, they may have too many points (especially if the edges are a bit noisy). 
approxPolyDP helps by giving you a simplified version of that contour with fewer points — which makes it easier to 
recognize shapes like triangles, rectangles, circles, etc.

🧪 How does it work?
cv2.approxPolyDP(curve, epsilon, closed)

📌 Parameters:

1) curve – The original contour (usually from cv2.findContours).

2) epsilon – The maximum distance/difference between the original curve and the simplified one.
   It ensures how much the approximated contour is simplied as caompared to orginal contour . Think of it as a tolerance:
   Smaller epsilon = more accurate (more points/corners)
   Larger epsilon = more simplified (fewer points/corners)

3) closed – Set to True if the shape is closed (like a square), False if it's open (like a line).
