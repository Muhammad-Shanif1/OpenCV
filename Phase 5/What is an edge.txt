🧠 What Is an Edge?
An edge in a picture is where things change — like the border between:
A black table and a white wall
A person’s face and the background
The edge of a shape like a circle or square
Edges help us understand where objects begin and end in a picture.

🧐 How Does a Computer "See" Edges?
A computer sees a picture as a grid of numbers — each number is a brightness level:

Pixels (brightness)	50	52	55	190	195

See how the numbers go from 55 to 190? That’s a big jump — and the computer thinks:
“Ah! Something changed here — this might be an edge!”

✅ So, To Detect Edges:

Look at each pixel
Compare it with its neighbors
If the difference is big → it's an edge
